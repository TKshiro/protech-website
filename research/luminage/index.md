# Luminage.co.jp Research

## Site Structure

Luminage is a Tokyo-based digital marketing agency (established 2013) specializing in online reputation management and inbound tourism marketing. The site is organized into four main sections:

- **Services** (サービス一覧): Divided into reputation risk management, LLMO/AI search optimization, and inbound marketing
- **Blog** (ブログ/COLUMN): Web marketing articles covering SEO, reputation management, LLMO, and inbound strategies
- **News** (ニュース): Company announcements, holiday notices, and press mentions
- **About**: Company overview, philosophy, history, and contact information

The blog uses a standard reverse-chronological listing with pagination. Each article shows title, date, category tag, and excerpt. Articles are categorized primarily as "ブログ" (Blog) or "ニュース" (News).

## Key Articles (小紅書/インバウンド Related)

### 訪日外国人が検索するキーワードの傾向は？海外版サジェスト対策についても解説

- **URL**: https://luminage.co.jp/blog/8090/
- **Date**: 2025-03-28 (Updated 2025-04-07)
- **Category**: Inbound Marketing / SEO Strategy
- **Key points**: 
  - International visitors search using four main patterns: location names, specific experiences/cuisine, quality/review terms, and cultural activities
  - Non-native speakers rely heavily on search suggestions, making suggest optimization crucial for hospitality businesses
  - Search suggestions vary by device language settings and browser configuration, requiring region-specific research
  - The company offers optimization across Yahoo, Google, Baidu, TikTok, RedNote, and Dianping
  - Success requires multilingual content, review management on tourism platforms like TripAdvisor, and targeted SEO around differentiating keywords
- **Image types used**: Stock photo of tourist/traveler in urban Japanese setting

### 訪日外国人のニーズは検索サジェストから読み解く？外国人観光客向けのサジェストマーケティングについて解説

- **URL**: https://luminage.co.jp/blog/8151/
- **Date**: 2025-04-09
- **Category**: Inbound Marketing / SEO Strategy
- **Key points**:
  - Google search suggestions reveal actual tourist needs rather than requiring guesswork
  - Four search behavior patterns: location-specific queries, experience-focused searches, convenience/rating terms, and seasonal/cultural events
  - International visitors use more sentence-based queries compared to Japanese users
  - Strategy involves analyzing suggestions in visitors' native languages by adjusting browser settings
  - For Chinese visitors, check Baidu and Dianping suggestions instead of Google
  - Case studies showed 25-30% booking increases after implementing these strategies
  - Requires multilingual website content, Google Business Profile optimization, and strengthening presence on review platforms
- **Image types used**: Generic illustration depicting international visitors and search suggestions (labeled "訪日外国人と検索サジェスト")

### 訪日中国人向けのインバウンドマーケティング（集客）はどうやって行う？中国でGoogleの代わりとなるサービスも解説

- **URL**: https://luminage.co.jp/blog/7049/
- **Date**: 2023-08-29 (Updated 2024-07-12)
- **Category**: Inbound Marketing / Digital Marketing Strategy
- **Key points**:
  - Standard Western marketing approaches don't work in China due to unique internet ecosystem
  - Four primary channels for reaching Chinese tourists:
    1. **Baidu**: China's dominant search engine requiring specialized SEO and localized keyword research
    2. **RED (Xiaohongshu)**: Hybrid social platform combining Instagram and Twitter features with 130+ million monthly active users; official accounts unlock partnership and e-commerce capabilities
    3. **WeChat**: Infrastructure-level messaging app serving 1+ billion users, essential for customer communication and transactions
    4. **Douyin**: China's domestic TikTok version (distinct from international app)
  - Generic website translations and domestic server hosting won't achieve results
  - Success requires understanding Chinese-specific search terminology, platform mechanics, and obtaining official account status on each channel
- **Image types used**: Screenshots of Baidu search interface, RED mobile app interface showing suggest feature, header image of Chinese tourists

### 百度（バイドゥ）での検索ボリュームはどのように調べれば良い？

- **URL**: https://luminage.co.jp/blog/5852/
- **Date**: 2021-05-26
- **Category**: Blog / China Marketing Strategy
- **Key points**:
  - Baidu lacks a direct search volume tool like Google's Keyword Planner
  - Baidu Index provides indexed data rather than absolute search numbers
  - Useful for comparing relative keyword demand but insufficient for ROI projections
  - The company offers access to local Chinese SEO tools through partnerships, enabling actual search volume data for clients planning China market entry
- **Image types used**: Two screenshots of Baidu Index interface showing data visualization and metrics display

## Service Pages (小紅書/Inbound Related)

### 小紅書 (RED/Xiaohongshu) Account Management

- **Target industries**: Restaurants, hotels, beauty/cosmetics, fashion, salons, clinics (hospitality and lifestyle sectors)
- **Pricing**: KOL marketing starts at ¥200,000; other services require consultation
- **Visuals**: Screenshots of rednote app interface, influencer posts, search suggestion examples

## Key Observations

1. **Service Positioning**: Luminage positions itself as a specialist in both reputation management (domestic focus) and Chinese inbound marketing (international focus)
2. **Pricing Transparency**: Chinese platform services have clear pricing (WeChat setup, Baidu SEO/ads, RED KOL marketing), while reputation services require consultation
3. **Expertise Demonstration**: Blog content serves as thought leadership, demonstrating deep knowledge of search algorithms, Chinese platforms, and industry-specific challenges
4. **Target Audience**: Japanese companies in hospitality, healthcare, real estate, and professional services sectors
5. **Content Frequency**: Regular blog updates (multiple per month) vs infrequent news updates (mainly holiday notices)
6. **Platform Emphasis**: Strong focus on 小紅書/RED as a key platform for reaching Chinese tourists, alongside WeChat and Baidu
7. **Barrier to Entry**: Emphasizes complexity of Chinese market (ICP registration, server requirements, language barriers, platform-specific rules) to justify service fees
