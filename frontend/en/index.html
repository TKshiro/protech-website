Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Can we communicate in Japanese?
Yes, absolutely. Our directors and project managers are bilingual in both Japanese and Chinese. They accurately understand the nuances of Japanese businesses and clearly relay your intentions to our local native Chinese staff, so you can rest assured.
Is a small budget or trial operation possible?
Yes, we offer flexible plans (e.g., KOL casting only, account management only) tailored to your budget and phase. We will propose the best plan during our free consultation and quote.
How long does it take to see results?
It depends on the product and campaign details. For SNS operations like RED (Xiaohongshu), clear changes (such as increase in reach and inquiries) typically start to appear in about 3 months, as initial account setup and content algorithm optimization progress.
Is there a fixed contract term?
For ongoing consulting services such as SNS account management, we ask for a minimum contract of "at least 3 months" or "at least 6 months" to ensure stable results. This does not apply to one-off influencer campaigns or creative production.
