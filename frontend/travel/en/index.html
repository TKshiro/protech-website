Compliance note

When accommodation, transportation, cars and attractions are arranged together, the service model should be checked against Japan travel business regulations and partner requirements before commercial launch.

  • No promise of guaranteed reservation success
  • No fabricated reviews
  • Private cars should use compliant providers
  • Cancellation and change rules must be clear
